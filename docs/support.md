# morrow support

*Published by Ibrahim, the developer of morrow.*

morrow keeps everything on your phone, so there's no account to sign in to and nothing we can look up or recover for you. Most questions are answered here.

**How do I move to a new phone?**
On the old phone, go to Settings > Data & privacy > Export a backup, choose a passphrase and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Files). On the new phone, install morrow and choose "Restore from a backup" on the welcome screen, or later in Settings > Data & privacy.

**I forgot my backup passphrase.**
It can't be recovered. The backup is encrypted with it and nobody, including us, can open it without it.

**The reminder didn't arrive.**
Check that notifications for morrow are allowed in your phone's settings. Battery saver and some phone makers' battery settings can delay reminders. morrow uses your phone's own scheduling, so exact delivery can't be guaranteed.

**I can't unlock morrow.**
App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or passcode. If your phone's lock has changed or been removed, follow the steps on morrow's locked screen.

**How do I delete everything?**
Settings > Data & privacy > Delete all data. This can't be undone.

**Is morrow medical advice?**
No. It's a personal log. If you're worried about your health, speak to a doctor.
