# morrow privacy policy

*Effective 13 November 2026. Published by Ibrahim, the developer of morrow.*

morrow is a personal log of bowel movements. It is built so that what you record stays with you.

## What morrow collects
**Nothing.** morrow has no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not use the internet, and the Android app doesn't ask for internet access. The developer never receives your entries or anything about how you use the app.

## What stays on your phone
These are stored only on your device, in morrow's private app storage:
- the entries you add (date, time if you record one, your note, and any optional details you choose to record, such as Bristol stool type, ease and context)
- your settings
- your reminder choice

The journal is encrypted on the device. Its key is kept in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain). morrow asks your phone to leave its data out of the phone's own cloud and device-transfer backups. Even if a copy were made, the journal couldn't be read without the key, which stays on this phone.

## Backups you make yourself
If you choose to export a backup:
- **Encryption:** morrow encrypts it with a passphrase you set.
- **Where it goes:** your phone's own save or share sheet takes it to wherever you pick, for example Files, Google Drive or iCloud Drive.
- **Who looks after it:** once saved, the file is in your care and that service's terms apply. morrow doesn't keep a copy.
- **Lost passphrase:** if you forget it, the backup can't be opened, by you or by us.

Restoring reads only the file you pick.

## Permissions
- **Notifications** (optional): only to show your weekly reminder, which says "Weekly check-in" and never includes your entries.
- **Fingerprint, face or passcode** (optional): only if you turn on app lock. morrow asks your phone to check you; it never sees your fingerprint or face data.
- **Start on restart** (Android): only so your reminder survives a restart.

## Deleting your data
Settings > Data & privacy > Delete all data removes every entry, setting, reminder and key from the phone. Uninstalling morrow also removes its data. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing for us to delete and no way for us to recover it.

## Children
morrow doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Not medical advice
morrow is a personal log, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, interpret or give health advice.
