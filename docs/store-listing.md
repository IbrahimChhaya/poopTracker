**Full description** (4,000 limit):

> morrow is a small, private journal for quick check-ins.
>
> **One tap is enough.** Add an entry and it's saved straight away, with today's date and time. Add a note or change the time if you like, or don't. Only the date is ever needed.
>
> **Look back by the week.** See this week at a glance, browse and search earlier entries, and view simple weekly counts over 4, 12 or 26 weeks. No scores, streaks or judgement.
>
> **Add detail only if you want it.** Optional extra details stay off until you turn them on, and turning them off never deletes anything.
>
> **A gentle weekly reminder.** Choose one day and time for a "Weekly check-in". The notification never shows what's in your journal.
>
> **Private by design.**
> • Your journal stays on this phone, encrypted.
> • No account, no sign-in, no cloud, no ads, no analytics.
> • morrow works entirely offline.
> • Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or phone passcode.
>
> **Your data, your backup.** Export an encrypted backup protected by a passphrase you choose, and save it wherever you like. Restore it on a new phone; entries already there are kept. Your passphrase can't be recovered, so keep it safe.
>
> **Delete everything, any time.** One clear step removes every entry, setting and reminder from the phone.
>
> morrow is a personal journal, not a medical or diagnostic tool.
